### Playstyle & Strategy
- **Philosophy:** Position-first, patience-based gameplay

- **Rotation Style:** Information-first, surgically precise zone rotations

### Weaknesses
❌ Lower fragging ceiling in late circles
❌ One secondary carry (Goblin) sometimes isn't enough against elite competition
❌ Positioning advantage narrows in final 3-4 teams

### Rotation Philosophy
> "Soul doesn't win because they outshoot everyone; they win because they're in the right position before anyone else realizes it's the right position."

- **Early Game:** Disciplined looting, avoid unnecessary fights
- **Mid Game:** Rotate to power positions, maintain zone control
- **Late Game:** Trust the macro, play for late circles
- **Key Trait:** Getting knocked down early doesn't change their fundamental approach

# UNIVERSAL ROTATION STRATEGIES
## Pro Player Rotation Guide (2026)

### Zone Timing Reference
| Zone | Time Before Shrinking | HP Damage/Second |
|------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Zone 1 | 3 minutes | 5 HP/sec |
| Zone 2 | 2 minutes | 10 HP/sec |
| Zone 3+ | 1.5 minutes each | 15 HP/sec |
| Final Zones | 1 minute each | 20-30 HP/sec |

---

### Early Game Rotation (Zone 1-2)
**Timing:**
- Rotate immediately if outside zone
- With 1 minute left if far
- Last 30 seconds if close

**Rules:**
- Avoid open fields
- Use natural cover (trees, rocks)
- Stay near edges, not center
- Listen for gunfights, avoid them
- Land with squad together
- Loot quickly, rotate before others arrive

---

### Mid Game Rotation (Zone 3-4)
**Positioning:**
- High ground near zone center
- Building with good sightlines
- Cover from multiple angles
- Escape route available

**Tactics:**
- Don't take unnecessary fights
- Let teams fight each other
- Push only when you have advantage
- Save resources for final circle
- Actively seek information about opponent positions
- Maintain zone control

---

### Late Game Rotation (Zone 5-6)
**Positioning:**
- Inside zone, never chasing
- High ground advantage
- Natural cover (rocks, slopes)
- 360° awareness

**Tactics:**
- Create instant cover while rotating
- Block enemy angles
- Safe healing spots
- Don't waste Gloo Walls early
- Coordination reaches peak level

---

### Final Circle Strategies (Zone 7-8)
**Positioning:**
- Center of circle
- Multiple Gloo Walls ready
- Team stays together
- Let others move first

**Last 2 Squads:**
- Know enemy position
- Use zone edge to your advantage
- Grenades to flush enemies
- Rush when they're weak

---

### Advanced Rotation Tricks

#### Edge Rotation
- Stay on zone edge initially
- Enemies focus on center fights
- Rotate to center in final zones
- Less combat, better survival

#### Fake Rotation
- Make noise in one direction
- Rotate different path
- Confuse enemy predictions
- Advanced tactic

#### Vehicle Rotation
- Use for long distances (>400m)
- Early game rotation
- Emergency zone chase
- Third-party escape
- **Avoid:** Final 3-4 circles (too loud)

#### Map-Specific Tips
- **Bermuda:** Avoid bridge chokes, use ziplines for fast rotation
- **Kalahari:** Use underground tunnels, control mill area
- **Avoid:** Open areas, center (Graveyard), mountain rotations when exposed

---

### Rotation Priority Checklist
- [ ] Check zone timer
- [ ] Plan path with cover
- [ ] Mark rotation for team
- [ ] Check inventory (meds/Gloo Walls)
- [ ] Start before others
- [ ] Find position, don't chase zone

---

### Team Rotation Coordination

#### Squad Movement Rules
- IGL calls rotation timing
- Move together, never split
- Cover each other while moving
- One scout ahead, three follow

#### Communication Calls
- "Rotate in 30 seconds"
- "Enemy blocking our path"
- "Taking high ground, cover me"
- "Zone pushing, let's move"

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Rotating too late (zone damage)
- Rotating too early (bad position)
- Fighting while rotating
- Not checking surroundings
- Running in open areas
