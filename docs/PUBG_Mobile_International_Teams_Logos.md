## Overview & Key Highlights

This document compiles the comprehensive roster of international PUBG Mobile esports teams tracked globally by Esports Charts. Unlike regional-only leagues (such as BGMI / KIE in India), these teams represent the premier global circuits including:
- **PMGC** (PUBG Mobile Global Championship)
- **PMWC / PMWI** (PUBG Mobile World Cup / World Invitational at Esports World Cup)
- **PMGO** (PUBG Mobile Global Open)
- **PMSL** (PUBG Mobile Super League: Southeast Asia, EMEA, Americas, Central & South Asia)
- **PEL** (Peacekeeper Elite League - China)
